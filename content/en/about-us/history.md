In February 2013, we were established as an organization. Long before that, since May 2012, Theresa Matengu, a high school teacher, had been feeding about 30 children out of her kitchen every Saturday. Helge Mercker offered to join her in the efforts of supporting the children in Drimiopsis. Shortly thereafter, dear friends in Canada, Australia, USA and other places began to generously support us by sending the money we desperately needed to expand our feeding program.
