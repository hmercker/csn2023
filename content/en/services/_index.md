### Our Objective

*To be engaged in rural communities, especially for the marginalized indigenous population:*

- to provide a safe and welcoming environment for orphaned, vulnerable and neglected children due to  HIV/Aids , illnesses, poverty, or other socio-economic circumstances;

- to create a safe environment for children who have been deprived of their basic human rights by building a Residential Child Care Facility ( children’s home and home of safety),

- to be engaged in facilitating re-integration of a child into family structure and its primary community;

- to provide a place of safety, proper medical assessments and care, having access to Schooling and Education, and
to have access to a balanced daily diet through produce from sustainable gardens.

*To empower orphans and vulnerable children*

By assisting in the upliftment of schooling of orphans and vulnerable children through involving them in educational programs eg afternoon study groups, computer literacy classes, walks in the veld with elders teaching traditional herbs and plants etc. thereby contributing towards their education for school and for life.  

By improving the living standard of poor and vulnerable children in our care (in the children’s home and home of safety) and in the community by providing for their basic needs, such as food, clothing, hostel- and school fees through donations or other means of support.

*To contribute to poverty alleviation by developing and running income –generating projects and job creation.*

Our presence in the community will indirectly contribute to poverty alleviation by generating jobs through engaging members of community  eg. Making bricks for the buildings, by employing members of community as house mothers and caregivers, by training and employing members to work in office of organisation, in the kitchen, in the self-sustaining gardens.

*To solicit funds national and internationally for the successful functioning of the organization.*

Our organisation operates in the Omaheke Region of Namibia and will lobby for funds nationally and internationally.
