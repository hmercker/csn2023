We are a registered welfare organization to assist orphaned and vulnerable children in Drimiopsis, in the Kalahari Constituency in the Omaheke Region of Namibia.

Our vision is, to make a difference, not only in the lives of children, but also whole communities and empowering marginalized communities in providing care for their young ones. Thus, assisting a self- empowered community to take care of their vulnerable, neglected, abused or orphaned children by honouring their basic human rights, by providing safety and protection, healing and love, and caring for their needs.
