Girls' Dormitory Room

Girls' dormitory room We joyfully share with you the exciting news that 16 children (8 boys, 8 girls) are now moving into the Mother House on the Sanctuary land! It will be a gradual move-in to help the children adjust from the lives they have known to a new life with many unknowns; having two dedicated caregivers, a place they can call their own, certainty of having their material and emotional needs met, relationships with other children who will become to them like siblings.
